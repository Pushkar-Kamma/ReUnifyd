FAQ

How do you connect to my channels?

Via YouTube’s official OAuth and Analytics APIs with read‑only scopes. You can revoke access anytime in Google Account Security.

Will you be able to post or edit videos?

No. The MVP is analytics‑only. No upload, edit, or publish permissions will be requested.

What does pricing look like?

We’re testing pricing with early users. Expect a free tier for very small channels, then paid tiers based on # of channels and data refresh frequency.

When is launch?

We’re assembling an alpha cohort now. Waitlist signups will be invited in small batches so we can iterate quickly on feedback.
